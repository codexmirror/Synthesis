VEYRA First Ordinary Phone — Visual Exploration V1

Human review of the first A/B/C exploration selected a specific synthesis rather than any one direction wholesale. The single final candidate is the human-selected B/C refinement below; the three original directions are kept as historical reference for how it was reached.

These files are design references, not Synthesis implementation. They are plain static HTML: they import nothing from src/, are not built, typechecked, tested or mounted by the Shell, and touch no GameState. The structural product contract they interpret is docs/design/VEYRA_FIRST_ORDINARY_PHONE_V1.md; the written comparison is docs/design/VEYRA_FIRST_ORDINARY_PHONE_VISUAL_EXPLORATION_V1.md.

SELECTED — human-selected B/C refinement The one final candidate. Direction C's visual language, typography, restraint and Settings / This Device; Direction B's product composition for Home, Communication and Money. Direction C's Home is rejected and replaced.

Historical exploration, superseded by the candidate above:

Direction A — “Index” Light warm paper. Hierarchy from type and whitespace only; hairlines are the sole structural device. Chrome appears only where it leads somewhere. Direction B — “Held” Warm graphite. Hierarchy from surface size and elevation; Domains are unequal objects and Settings deliberately has no surface. Navigation is a physical control object. Direction C — “Frame” Domain grounds. Home never scrolls: three unequal full-bleed regions whose proportions are the hierarchy. Entering a Domain expands its ground to fill the frame.

Each direction page can be reduced to a single screen at a chosen width — useful for checking the design on a real phone — with a query string, for example direction-c.html?only=home&w=320. Valid only values are home, communication, conversation, money, settings and settings-device.
